The Gap This Closes

Odoo produces the invoice it emails by a different route than the one the Print button uses.

Without this module

🔓

You switch password protection on for the invoice report. Printing gives you a protected file. Send & Print quietly sends your customer an unprotected one - the exact copy you most wanted covered.

With this module

🔒

Both routes deliver the same protected document. Because invoices are handled one at a time, every customer's copy is locked with that customer's own password.

Install This One Second

This is an add-on. It extends a free module rather than replacing it.

1. PDF Password Protection

The free module this one builds on. It is where you switch protection on, choose the encryption strength, and decide whether the password is fixed or taken from each customer's VAT number, phone or email. Search the Apps store for PDF Password Protection.

2. This add-on

Extends those same settings to the invoice Odoo emails. It installs itself automatically once both PDF Password Protection and Accounting are present, and it has no settings of its own.

You can use PDF Password Protection on its own. You cannot use this one without it.

What It Does Not Do

⚠  It protects the copy that leaves, not the one you keep

The invoice is locked as it enters the customer's email and as the portal hands it over. The copy on the record stays readable, so your own team can open a sent invoice without knowing the customer's password.

⚠  Structured e-invoices are left readable

Where Odoo produces a legally structured e-invoice, locking it would break the format and stop your customer's system reading it. Those are sent unprotected on purpose, with a note on the invoice explaining why.

✗  It is not the settings screen

Whether protection is on, which password is used and how strong the encryption is are all decided in PDF Password Protection. This add-on only makes sure the emailed invoice obeys them.

✗  It does not invent a password

The notice tells the customer a password is needed; it never contains the password itself. Agreeing that with your customer stays your job.
